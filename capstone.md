# Apply Capstone Additions & Enhancements: 

## Work From Home Policy (Added to hr_policy.md)

- Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week, subject to manager approval.
- WFH requests should be communicated at least 1 day in advance (except emergencies).
- Reachable during core hours: 10 AM - 5 PM. Does not count against casual/sick leave.
- New employees (first 30 days) work from office to support onboarding.

## Probation Period Leave Rules (Added to hr_policy.md)

- Probation employees (first 90 days) accrue leave but require manager approval to use (except sick leave).
- Sick leave follows standard employee rules. Unused leave carries over upon confirmation.

## Additional Disciplinary Guardrail (Added to guardrail_rules.md)

- Never comment on, speculate about, or provide info regarding ongoing HR investigations, disciplinary proceedings, or grievance cases.
- Refusal Response: "That falls under an area I'm not able to discuss - ongoing HR matters like this are kept strictly confidential. Please reach out to hr@artscape.com directly for anything related to this."
