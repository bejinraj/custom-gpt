# Artscape Employee Handbook-Leave, Holidays & Reimbursements

## Leave Policy

- Every full-time employee is entitled to 12 casual leaves (CL) per calendar year.
- Employees also get 8 sick leaves (SL) per calendar year.
- Leaves reset on January 1st each year and do not carry forward to the next year.

- New employees accrue leave at 1 casual leave per completed month of employment during their first year.
- Casual leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 2 working days in advance.
- Sick leave can be applied for on the same day, but a doctor's note is required for sick leave lasting more than 2 consecutive days.
- Unused leaves are not carried forward and are not paid out at year-end.

## Reimbursement Process

1. Log in to the Finance Portal at finance.artscape.internal.
2. Click "New Reimbursement Request".
3. Select a category: Travel, Food, Internet, or Office Supplies.
4. Upload a clear photo or scan of the original receipt.
5. Enter the amount and a short description.
6. Submit the request -it goes to your manager for approval.
7. Once approved, reimbursement is processed within 7 working days and credited to your registered bank account.

## Reimbursement limits:

- Travel: up to ₹5,000/month without prior approval.
- Intemnet: up to ₹1,000/month.
- Food (while traveling for work): up to ₹800/day.

Status tracking: Employees can check reimbursement status anytime under "My Requests" in the Finance Portal. Statuses are:
Submitted -+ Manager Review-+ Approved -+ Paid.
