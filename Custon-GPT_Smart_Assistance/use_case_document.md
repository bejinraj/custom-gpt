# Use Case Document - Custom GPT

## GPT Name
HR Buddy

## Target Users
- ** Primary users :** All employees at Artscape who need quick answers about leave, holidays, and reimbursements.
** Secondary users :** New hires during onboarding, who often don't know where to find HR policies.

## Problem Statement
Employees frequently message the HR and IT teams to ask about leave balances, the holiday calendar, and reimbursement status. This creates repeated interruptions for HR staff and delays responses for employees, since the same handful of questions are answered manually, one at a time, every day.

## Expected Outcomes
Reduce repetitive HR/IT queries by 50% within 2 months of launch.
- Cut average response time for these queries from 4 hours (human reply) to under 1 minute (GPT reply).
Achieve 80% of employees using the GPT instead of messaging HR directly for these 3 topics.
Free up ~5 hours per week of HR staff time currently spent answering repetitive questions.
