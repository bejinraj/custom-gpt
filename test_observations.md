1. To submit a reimbursement request, follow these steps:

1. Log in to the Finance Portal at finance.artscape.internal.

1. Click "New Reimbursement Request".

1. Select a category: Travel, Food, Internet, or Office Supplies.

1. Upload a clear photo or scan of the original receipt.

1. Enter the amount and a short description.

1. Submit the request so it goes to your manager for approval.

Once approved, the reimbursement is processed within 7 working days and credited to your registered bank account.

Let me know if you need help with anything else or have any questions about reimbursement limits!

2. Full-time employees are entitled to **12 casual leaves (CL)** and **8 sick leaves (SL)** per calendar year.

Here are a few quick details about taking leave:

- **Casual Leave:** Must be submitted through the HR portal at least 2 working days in advance. New employees accrue 1 casual leave per completed month of employment during their first year.

- **Sick Leave:** Can be applied for on the same day, though a doctor's note is required if it lasts for more than 2 consecutive days.

- **Reset & Carry Forward:** Leaves reset on January 1st each year and do not carry forward or pay out at year-end.

Let me know if you need help with anything else or have questions about submitting a leave request!
